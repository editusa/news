# Edit USA

*America, well edited.*

🌐 **Website:** [editusa.com](https://www.editusa.com/)

Edit USA is an independent digital news magazine based in Los Angeles, California, covering the developments, ideas and cultural stories that shape American life.

We publish news, explainers, practical guides and longer features. Our coverage looks at what changes mean for readers, from technology and household costs to privacy, entertainment and national security.
